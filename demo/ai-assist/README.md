# AI Assist - asking a broker in your own words

[`c3-ai-assist.mp4`](c3-ai-assist.mp4) - 89 s, 1920×1080. Explore on a factory floor (`examples/factory.sh`,
its robots at work with `examples/live.sh`); AI Assist asked, in turn: *Show only the robots and their
docks* - *Which robots have a battery below 40 %? Highlight them* - *Paint the robots blue* - one change
undone by hand with its line's **Undo** - *Undo everything else*.

[`index.html`](index.html) plays it with a pause after each prompt is typed and after each answer - any key
or a click goes on (a checkbox turns the pauses off), and the prompts as chapters. Open it from a checkout
(`demo/ai-assist/index.html`).

AI Assist is not in c³ yet: what the panel will ask for is in its popup (**✦ AI Assist**).
