AI Assist coming soon

Ask about the connected broker in your own words - which vehicles near the depot were above 90 today? - and c³ answers by querying it, with the entities of the answer highlighted in Explore.

When it comes, this is where you set it up:

  • Provider and model - the model is reached with your own key; c³ is the client.
  • API key - kept by c³'s backend on this machine (with remember, beside c3.json, mode 600), never sent to the page. Or from the environment when c³ starts.
  • A monthly limit - and the tokens each answer used, shown under it.

The broker's own access token and tenant - Token and the tenant field in Explore - are what the assistant uses: it can do exactly what you can, and asks you before anything it would change.

The Data Space Connector: the provider above is the consumer's Keycloak; c³ gets the credential there and presents it to the verifier.

          

            
            

              
              
            

          

          

            
            
          

        

        
Or a token of its own

        

          
          
          

            
            
          

        

        
Sent as Authorization: Bearer with every request to this broker - reads, live values, writes. The broker decides what it allows. A login comes first, and its token is renewed by c³ until the provider ends the session; the provider's client must be a public one that allows http://<c³>/api/oidc/callback as a redirect URI. Without a login or a token of its own, a broker gets c³'s default: --token-file, else $C3_TOKEN.
